# Graph Minors XXII: corrections

N. Robertson and P. D. Seymour, *Graph Minors. XXII. Irrelevant vertices in linkage problems*,
J. Combin. Theory Ser. B **102** (2012), 530–563.

A corrected copy of the paper, with each correction below marked in place, is
[GM22/GM22.pdf](https://github.com/hajiaghayi/GraphMinorPapers/blob/main/GM22/GM22.pdf) in the
repository [GraphMinorPapers](https://github.com/hajiaghayi/GraphMinorPapers).

The corrections are in the order of the pages.

## 1. Proof of 2.4, page 532: the proof

**Kind.** Missing proof.

**What is missing.** Before 2.4 the paper says "We also need the following, and we leave its proof
to the reader." The proof adds $e$ back to a subgraph with the same effect as $L \setminus \{e\}$
and uses that $L$ is vital.

**Proof.** *Proof.* Let $e$ have ends $u, v$; thus $u, v \in Z$. Certainly
$Z \subseteq V(L) = V(L \setminus \{e\})$. By 2.2, $L$ is a forest, and so $u$ and $v$ belong to
different components of $L \setminus \{e\}$. Now let $L' \subseteq G$ have the same effect on $Z$ as
$L \setminus \{e\}$; we must show that $L' = L \setminus \{e\}$. Since $u, v \in Z$, it follows that
$u, v \in V(L')$ and that they belong to different components of $L'$; and in particular
$e \notin E(L')$. Let $L''$ be the subgraph of $G$ obtained from $L'$ by adding $e$, so that
$L'' \setminus \{e\} = L'$. For $x, y \in Z$, $x$ and $y$ belong to the same component of $L''$ if
and only if either they belong to the same component of $L'$, or one of them belongs to the same
component of $L'$ as $u$ and the other to the same component of $L'$ as $v$; and the same holds with
$L'', L'$ replaced by $L, L \setminus \{e\}$. Since $L'$ and $L \setminus \{e\}$ have the same
effect on $Z$, and $u, v \in Z$, it follows that $L''$ and $L$ have the same effect on $Z$. Since
$L$ is vital for $Z$, we deduce that $L'' = L$, and so
$L' = L'' \setminus \{e\} = L \setminus \{e\}$. This proves 2.4. $\square$

**Lean.** `GraphMinor.GM22.theorem_2_4_proof` in `GraphMinor/GM22/Section2Basic.lean`, restated as
`GraphMinor.GM22.theorem_2_4` in `GraphMinor/GM22/Results.lean`. The use of 2.2 is
`GraphMinor.GM22.theorem_2_2_proof` in the same file.

## 2. Claim (7) in the proof of 3.1, page 536: $w$, not $\omega$

**Kind.** Misprint.

**What goes wrong.** The proof of Claim (7) ends "Thus $2t \le \omega + 1$". The sentence before
says that if $2t \ge w + 2$, then by 3.2 the graph $G$ has tree-width at least $w + 1$, contrary to
(6) and 2.1. Claim (7) itself says "at most $\frac12(w + 1)$". In this paper, $\omega$ is used for
the sequence of roots of a rooted digraph, not for a number.

**Correction.** The sentence reads "Thus $2t \le w + 1$". The statement is not affected.

## 3. Section 5, definition of a respectful tangle, page 539: $\Gamma \cap \overline{\Sigma \setminus \Delta}$

**Kind.** Misprint.

**What goes wrong.** A tangle $\mathcal{T}$ in a drawing $\Gamma$ is called respectful if for every
$\Gamma$-normal O-arc $F$ with $|F \cap V(\Gamma)| < ord(\mathcal{T})$ there is a closed disc
$\Delta$ bounded by $F$ with
"$(\Gamma \cap \Delta, \Sigma \cap \overline{\Sigma \setminus \Delta}) \in \mathcal{T}$". The
members of $\mathcal{T}$ are separations of $\Gamma$, and
$\Sigma \cap \overline{\Sigma \setminus \Delta}$ is a set of points, not a subgraph of $\Gamma$.

**Correction.** The condition reads
$(\Gamma \cap \Delta, \Gamma \cap \overline{\Sigma \setminus \Delta}) \in \mathcal{T}$.

**Lean.** `GraphMinor.GM22.IsTangleIns` in `GraphMinor/GM22/Defs.lean`. The second term is
`GraphMinor.GM11.PaintingOrDrawing.interOutside` in `GraphMinor/GM11/Defs.lean`.

## 5. Statement of 5.1, page 539: $z \in A(\Gamma)$

## 6. Statement of 6.3, page 543: the roots of $G'$

**Kind.** Statement mistake.

**What goes wrong.** 6.3 is about rooted digraphs $(G, u_1, \dots, u_q)$, $(G', u'_1, \dots, u'_q)$
and $(H, v_1, \dots, v_q)$, a $Z$-division $\{A_0, \dots, A_k\}$ of $G$ and a $Z'$-division
$\{A'_0, \dots, A'_k\}$ of $G'$. It asks that "$\alpha \colon Z' \to Z$ is a function mapping $Z'$
onto $Z$ and for $1 \le i \le k$ mapping $\pi'_i$ to $\pi_i$". Nothing ties the roots of $G'$ to the
roots of $G$. But the proof of Claim (9), page 545, uses that $\alpha(u'_i) = u_i$. The statement is
false without it.

For example, take $q = k = 1$ and $\delta = 0$. Let $G = G'$ have two vertices $a, b$ and no edges,
with root $u_1 = u'_1 = a$, and let $H$ be one vertex $v_1$, its root. Let $Z = Z' = \{a, b\}$,
$A_0 = A'_0 = G$, $A_1 = (\{a\}, \emptyset)$ with $\pi_1 = (a)$, and $A'_1 = (\{b\}, \emptyset)$
with $\pi'_1 = (b)$. Let $\alpha$ swap $a$ and $b$, let $\phi(v_1) = A_1$, and let $L'_0 = G'$.
Every printed hypothesis holds. In particular, $(A_1, \pi_1)$ and $(A'_1, \pi'_1)$ are each one root
and nothing else, so they have the same $0$-folio. Also $L \cap A_0$ and $L'_0$ both have no edges,
so two vertices of $Z'$ are $L'_0$-connected if and only if their images are
$(L \cap A_0)$-connected. But a model $\phi'$ of $(H, v_1)$ in $(G', a)$ would need $\phi'(v_1)$ to
be connected, to contain $a$ and to meet $A'_1 = (\{b\}, \emptyset)$, and $a, b$ are not joined in
$G'$.

**Correction.** Add the hypothesis $\alpha(u'_j) = u_j$ for $1 \le j \le q$. With it, 6.3 holds, and
the step in Claim (9) is justified. The paper uses 6.3 once, in Claim (12) in the proof of 7.3, page
551. There $u_j = u'_j = w_j \in Z_0$, and $\alpha(v) = v$ for $v \in Z_0$ (page 550), so the added
hypothesis holds and the later results are not affected.

**Lean.** `GraphMinor.GM22.theorem_6_3` in `GraphMinor/GM22/Results.lean`, proved by
`GraphMinor.GM22.theorem_6_3_proof` in `GraphMinor/GM22/Section6.lean`. The example above is
`GraphMinor.GM22.not_theorem_6_3_without_roots` in `GraphMinor/GM22/Results.lean`, which proves that
the printed statement is false. The use in 7.3 is `GraphMinor.GM22.lemma_7_3_claim_12` in
`GraphMinor/GM22/Section7c4.lean`.

## 7. Proof of 6.3, page 543: $P_j$ and $p_j$
