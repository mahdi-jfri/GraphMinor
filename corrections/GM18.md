# Graph Minors XVIII: corrections

N. Robertson and P. D. Seymour, *Graph Minors. XVIII. Tree-decompositions and well-quasi-ordering*,
J. Combin. Theory Ser. B **89** (2003), 77–108.

A corrected copy of the paper, with each correction below marked in place, is
[GM18/GM18.pdf](https://github.com/hajiaghayi/GraphMinorPapers/blob/main/GM18/GM18.pdf) in the
repository [GraphMinorPapers](https://github.com/hajiaghayi/GraphMinorPapers).

The corrections are in the order of the pages.

## 1. Proof of 2.1, page 79: the proof

**Kind.** Missing proof.

**What is missing.** After 2.1 the paper says "The proof is clear." No proof is given. The proof
checks the three axioms (T1)–(T3) for $\mathscr{T}$ by restricting each separation of $G$ to $G'$.

**Proof.** To see this, let $(A, B)$ be a separation of $G$ of order $<\theta$. Then
$(A \cap G', B \cap G')$ is a separation of $G'$, since $(A \cap G') \cup (B \cap G') = G'$ and
$E(A \cap B) = \emptyset$, and its order is at most $|V(A \cap B)| < \theta$. So by (T1) for
$\mathscr{T}'$, one of $(A \cap G', B \cap G')$, $(B \cap G', A \cap G')$ belongs to $\mathscr{T}'$,
and therefore $\mathscr{T}$ contains one of $(A, B)$, $(B, A)$. This proves (T1) for $\mathscr{T}$.
If $(A_i, B_i) \in \mathscr{T}$ $(i = 1, 2, 3)$ and $A_1 \cup A_2 \cup A_3 = G$, then
$(A_1 \cap G') \cup (A_2 \cap G') \cup (A_3 \cap G') = G'$, contrary to (T2) for $\mathscr{T}'$. If
$(A, B) \in \mathscr{T}$ and $V(A) = V(G)$, then $V(A \cap G') = V(G')$, contrary to (T3) for
$\mathscr{T}'$. Thus $\mathscr{T}$ satisfies (T1)–(T3), and so it is a tangle in $G$ of order
$\theta$.

**Lean.** `GraphMinor.GM18.theorem_2_1_proof` in `GraphMinor/GM18/Section2.lean`, restated as
`GraphMinor.GM18.theorem_2_1` in `GraphMinor/GM18/Results.lean`.

## 2. Proof of 2.4, page 80: the proof

**Kind.** Missing proof.

**What is missing.** Before 2.4 the paper says "The following is easily seen to be true (compare
Theorem 9.1 of [3])." No proof of 2.4 is given. The proof must check that each pair is a separation,
and that $\tau \times T_i \subseteq \tau \times T'_j$ for distinct members of the set.

**Proof.** *Proof.* For $1 \le i \le k$, let $u_i$ be the end of $e_i$ different from $t_0$. Since
every vertex of $T$ belongs to one of $T_i, T'_i$, the first condition in the definition of a
tree-decomposition implies that $\tau \times T_i \cup \tau \times T'_i = G$; and since $T_i, T'_i$
are disjoint, the second condition implies that
$E(\tau \times T_i \cap \tau \times T'_i) = \emptyset$. So each
$(\tau \times T_i, \tau \times T'_i)$ is a separation of $G$. Now let $1 \le i, j \le k$ with
$(\tau \times T_i, \tau \times T'_i) \ne (\tau \times T_j, \tau \times T'_j)$. Then $i \ne j$, and
so $u_i \ne u_j$. We claim that $V(T_i) \subseteq V(T'_j)$. For let $s \in V(T_i)$. Since $s$ and
$t_0$ belong to different components of $T \setminus e_i$, the path of $T$ between $s$ and $t_0$
uses $e_i$, and so $u_i$ is its vertex next to $t_0$. If $s \notin V(T'_j)$, then in the same way
$u_j$ is the vertex of this path next to $t_0$, and so $u_i = u_j$, a contradiction. This proves our
claim. Consequently $\tau \times T_i \subseteq \tau \times T'_j$, and so the set in 2.4 is a
location. This proves 2.4. $\square$

**Lean.** `GraphMinor.GM18.theorem_2_4_proof` in `GraphMinor/GM18/Section2.lean`, restated as
`GraphMinor.GM18.theorem_2_4` in `GraphMinor/GM18/Results.lean`. The separations are
`GraphMinor.GM18.isSeparation_treeDecompSide`, and the step about the path between $s$ and $t_0$
uses `GraphMinor.GM18.reachable_deleteEdges_iff_onTreePath` in `GraphMinor/GM18/Section2.lean` and
`GraphMinor.Thomas.onTreePath_adj_dichotomy` in `GraphMinor/Thomas/Basic.lean`.

## 8. Proof of 6.5, page 104: the proof of Claim (3)

**Kind.** Missing proof.

**What is missing.** After Claim (3) the paper says "The proof of (3) is very similar to that of (2)
and we leave it to the reader." No proof is given.

**Proof.** *Subproof.* Let $t$ be the head of $f_0$, which is the tail of $f_1$. Then
$T^{f_1} \subseteq T^t = T^{f_0}$, and so
$(\tau \times T^{f_1})^- \subseteq (\tau \times T^{f_0})^-$. By 6.5.2, $G \setminus G_0$ is the
rooted hypergraph $H$ with $H^- = (\tau \times T^{f_0})^-$, and so
$\tau \times T^{f_1} \subseteq G \setminus G_0$. For the second statement, $T_{f_0}$ consists of the
vertices of $T$ not in $T^{f_0}$, and $T_{f_1}$ of those not in $T^{f_1}$; since
$V(T^{f_1}) \subseteq V(T^{f_0})$, every vertex of $T_{f_0}$ belongs to $T_{f_1}$. Hence
$G_0^- = (\tau \times T_{f_0})^- \subseteq (\tau \times T_{f_1})^-$. By 6.5.1,
$G \setminus \tau \times T^{f_1} = \tau \times T_{f_1}$, and so
$G_0 \subseteq G \setminus \tau \times T^{f_1}$. This proves (3).

**Lean.** `GraphMinor.GM18.lemma_6_5_claim_3` in `GraphMinor/GM18/Section6Part1.lean`, which uses
`GraphMinor.GM18.theorem_6_5_1` and `GraphMinor.GM18.theorem_6_5_2` in the same file.

## 9. Proof of 6.6, page 106: the bound on the number of roots

**Kind.** Proof gap.

**What goes wrong.** To apply Theorem (3.3) of Graph Minors IV to the stars of the arborescences
$T_i$, the proof needs one $k$ with $|\bar{\pi}(t)| \le k$ for all the stars. It says
that "since $\mathscr{C}$ is well-behaved, there exists $k \ge 0$ such that
$|\bar{\pi}(G)| \le k$ for every $(G, \mu, \Delta, \phi) \in \mathscr{C}$". But every member of
$\mathscr{C}$ is rootless, by the hypothesis of 6.6, so this bound is just $k = 0$. For a vertex $t$
of $T_i$ other than its root, the roots of $\tau_i(t)$ are those of a rooting of a member of
$\mathscr{C}$, as in Claim (1) of the proof. Their number is the order of the edge of $T_i$ that
enters $t$, and this can be positive. So the bound over $\mathscr{C}$ does not cover them.

**Correction.** Take the bound over $\mathscr{C}^*$, the union of $\mathscr{C}$ and the class of all
rootings of members of $\mathscr{C}$, which the proof defines on page 104. As in Claim (2) of the
proof, $\mathscr{C}^*$ is well-behaved by 6.1. So there is $k \ge 0$ with $|\bar{\pi}(G)| \le k$
for every $(G, \mu, \Delta, \phi) \in \mathscr{C}^*$. For every $i$ and every $t \in V(T_i)$, the
roots of $\tau_i(t)$ are those of a member of $\mathscr{C}$ or of a rooting of one, so
$|\bar{\pi}(t)| \le k$. 6.6 holds as printed.

**Lean.** `GraphMinor.GM18.theorem_6_6` in `GraphMinor/GM18/Results.lean`. The step above is in
`GraphMinor.GM18.theorem_6_6_of_rootedDecomposition` in `GraphMinor/GM18/Section6Stars.lean`. It
applies `GraphMinor.GM18.WellBehaved.exists_roots_length_le` to
`GraphMinor.GM18.rootingClosure`, the class $\mathscr{C}^*$, which is well-behaved by
`GraphMinor.GM18.wellBehaved_rootingClosure`. These three are in
`GraphMinor/GM18/Section6Part2.lean`.
